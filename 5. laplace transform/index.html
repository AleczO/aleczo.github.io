Laplace Transform

Introduction

This post is my notes and thoughts about Laplace transform. You can find here definition basic properties and interesting usage examples.


Definition

Let $f(t)$ be a function defined for $t \in \mathbb{R}_+$. Then the function

$$ F(s) = \mathcal{L}[f(t)] = \int_0^{\infty}e^{-st} f(t) dt $$

Is called Laplace Transform where $s \in \mathbb{C}$.


Important Theorems and Properties


1. Linearity

$$\mathcal{L}[\alpha f(t) + \beta g(t)] = \alpha F(t) + \beta G(t) $$
$$ \mathcal{L}[\alpha f(t) + \beta g(t)] $$ $$ = \int_0^{\infty} (\alpha f(t) + \beta g(t))e^{-st} dt $$ $$ = \int_0^{\infty} \alpha f(t)e^{-st} dt + \int_0^{\infty} \beta g(t)e^{-st} dt $$ $$ = \alpha \int_0^{\infty} f(t)e^{-st} dt + \beta \int_0^{\infty} g(t)e^{-st} dt $$ $$ = \alpha F(t) + \beta G(t) $$

2. Transform of First Derivative

$$ \mathcal{L}[f'(t)] = s F(s) - f(0) $$
$$ \mathcal{L}[f'(x)] $$ $$ = \int_0^{\infty} f'(t) e^{-st} dt $$ $$ = f(t)e^{-st} \bigg|_0^{\infty} + s\int_0^{\infty} f(t)e^{-st} dt $$ $$ = \lim_{t \rightarrow \infty} f(t)e^{-st} - f(0)e^{-s \cdot 0} + s\int_0^{\infty} f(t)e^{-st} dt $$

assuming $\Re(s) > 0$

$$ = -f(0) + sF(t) $$

3. Transform of Second Derivative

$$ \mathcal{L}[f''(t)] = s^2 F(s) -s f(0) - f'(0) $$

From property 2 we get

$$ \mathcal{L}[f''(t)] = s\mathcal{L}[f'(t)] - f'(0) $$ $$ = s[s\mathcal{L}[f(t)] - f(0)] - f'(0) $$ $$ = s^2\mathcal{L}[f(t)] - sf(0) - f'(0) $$ $$ = s^2 F(t) - sf(0) - f'(0) $$

4. Transform of $n$-th derivative

$$ \mathcal{L}[f^{(n)}(t)] = s^n F(s) - s^{n-1} f(0) - s^{n-2} f'(0) - \dots - sf^{(n -2)}(0) - f^{(n - 1)} (0) $$ $$ = s^n F(s) - \sum_{k=1}^n s^{n-k} f^{(k - 1)} (0) $$
$$ \mathcal{L}[f^{(n)}(t)] $$ $$ = s\mathcal{L}[f^{(n-1)}(t)] - f^{(n - 1)}(0) $$ $$ = s^2 \mathcal{L}[f^{(n-2)}(t)] - sf^{(n-2)} (0) - f^{(n - 1)}(0) $$ $$ = s^3 \mathcal{L}[f^{(n-3)}(t)] - s^2 f^{(n-3)}(0) - sf^{(n-2)}(0) - f^{(n-1)}(0) $$ $$ \vdots $$ $$ = s^n \mathcal{L}[f(t)] - s^{n-1} f(0) - s^{n-2}f'(0) - \dots - sf^{(n-2)}(0) - f^{(n-1)}(0) $$

5. Transform of the Integral

$$ \mathcal{L} \bigg[ \int_0^t f(\tau) d \tau \bigg] = \frac{1}{s} F(s) $$

6. Multiplication Function by $t$

$$ \mathcal{L}[t f(t)] = - \frac{dF(s)}{ds} $$

7. Dividing Function by $t$

$$ \mathcal{L} \bigg[\frac{f(t)}{t} \bigg] = \int_s^{\infty} F(s) ds $$

8. Time Scaling

$$ \mathcal{L} [f(at)] = \frac{1}{a} F\big( \frac{s}{a} \big) $$

9. Time Shift

$$ \mathcal{L}[f(t - T)] = e^{-sT} F(s) $$

10. Frequency Shift

$$ \mathcal{L}{f(t)e^{-at}} = F(s + a) $$

11. Convolution

$$ \mathcal{L}[f(t) \ast g(t)] = F(s) \cdot G(s) $$
